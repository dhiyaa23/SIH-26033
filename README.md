## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is AgriConnect, a digital platform that helps farmers sell their products directly to wholesalers, retailers, supermarkets, restaurants and other bulk buyers.
Nowadays, farmers often have to depend on middlemen to sell their products. Because of this, they may not always get a fair price for their crops. Through AgriConnect, we aim to reduce this dependency and make it easier for farmers to connect with buyers directly.
In this platform, farmers can upload details about the crops they have, including the quantity and expected price. Buyers can check the available products, compare prices and place orders according to their needs.
Small farmers who have limited quantities can also join together and sell their products as a group. This helps them fulfil large orders that they may not be able to handle individually.

Key Features:
1. Farmer Registration: Farmers can create an account and add details about their crops, quantity, location and expected price.
2. Buyer Registration: Wholesalers, retailers, supermarkets and restaurants can register and mention the products they need.
3. Bulk Order Management: Buyers can place large orders directly with farmers or farmer groups.
4. AI-Based Buyer Matching: AI helps farmers find suitable buyers by checking the crop type, quantity, location and price.
5. Live Market Price Updates: Farmers can view market prices and use this information while deciding their selling price.
6. Digital Price Negotiation: Farmers and buyers can communicate through the platform and discuss prices before confirming an order.
7. Farmer Group Formation: Small farmers can join together and combine their products to meet the requirements of bulk buyers.
8. Order and Payment Tracking: Farmers and buyers can check their order details, payment records and delivery status.

Through AgriConnect, we aim to make agricultural trading easier and more transparent. It also gives farmers better opportunities to find buyers and negotiate prices

## Technical Approach
AgriConnect will be developed as a web application using basic web technologies, Python and AI-based features.
The platform will have separate sections for farmers and buyers, where they can register, upload product details, search for products and manage their orders.

Technologies Used:
   Component                   Technology
1. Frontend                 HTML, CSS, JavaScript
2. Backend                  Python (Flask)
3. Database                 MySQL
4. AI/ML                    Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn
5. Maps                     OpenStreetMap or Google Maps API

Implementation Steps:
1. Step 1 – User Registration:
Farmers and buyers can register on the platform by entering their basic details. Separate accounts will be provided for both users.
2. Step 2 – Product Listing:
Farmers can upload their crop details, including the crop name, quantity, expected price, location and product images.
3. Step 3 – Buyer Requirements:
Buyers can post the products they need, along with the required quantity, preferred price and delivery location.
4. Step 4 – AI-Based Buyer Matching:
The system checks the details provided by farmers and buyers. Based on crop type, quantity, location and price, it suggests suitable buyers to farmers.
5. Step 5 – Bulk Order Management:
Farmers who have smaller quantities of crops can form groups and combine their products to fulfil large orders.
6. 7. Step 6 – Price Comparison and Negotiation:
The platform displays available market price information. Farmers and buyers can compare prices and negotiate before finalising the deal.
Step 7 – Order Management:
After an order is confirmed, both the farmer and buyer can view the order details, payment information and delivery progress.

AI Implementation:
1. AI and machine learning can be used in the following ways:
2. To suggest suitable buyers for farmers based on their crop details.
3. To match buyer requirements with available agricultural products.
4. To study previous market prices and identify price trends.
5. To suggest suitable selling prices based on available market information

## Feasibility and Viability
Technical Feasibility:
AgriConnect can be developed using commonly available technologies such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and MySQL.
These technologies are suitable for developing a web application. AI features like buyer matching and price suggestions can be developed using Python libraries such as Scikit-learn.

Operational Feasibility:
The platform will have separate interfaces for farmers and buyers, making it easier for them to use the system.
Simple navigation, regional language support and easy product uploading can help farmers who are not familiar with digital platforms.

Economic Feasibility:
The initial version of AgriConnect can be developed using free and open-source tools.
Since the project does not require expensive infrastructure during the prototype stage, the development cost can be kept low.

Business Viability:
1. AgriConnect can generate revenue through different methods, such as:
2. Charging a small service fee for successful transactions.
3. providing subscription plans for large-scale buyers.
4. Partnering with Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).
5. Offering premium market analysis services.
6. Earning commissions through logistics partnerships.

## Research and References
Research:
Before developing AgriConnect, we looked into existing agricultural marketing platforms and the methods used to connect farmers with buyers.
The following concepts helped us understand how our proposed system can work.

1. National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)
e-NAM is an online platform that connects agricultural markets and supports transparent price discovery and online trading. It shows how digital platforms can help improve agricultural marketing.
2. Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)
FPOs help farmers come together and work as a group. By combining their resources and products, farmers can improve their bargaining power and reach larger markets.
3. Digital Agricultural Marketing
Digital platforms help farmers communicate with buyers and access markets beyond their local areas. This can create more opportunities for farmers to sell their products.
4. AI-Based Buyer Matching
AI and machine learning can be used to identify suitable buyers based on crop type, quantity, location and price. This can make it easier for farmers to find buyers who match their requirements.
5. Agricultural Price Information
Market price information helps farmers compare prices and make better decisions about when and where to sell their products
